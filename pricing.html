Say your i‑do’s with me

What’s included

Every collection is built around your day, so the final number depends on hours, locations and travel. Here is what comes with me either way. Full pricing arrives in your custom guide.

  1. 01

    Elopement photography

    For couples ready to break tradition and do things their way. Run through the NYC streets or hike the mountains in Malibu. No matter the season, we’ll customize the day around the two of you.

  2. 02

    Intimate wedding photography

    Small guest lists, big feeling. Courthouse ceremonies, rooftop dinners, a few family members by your side. Your day should feel intentional and just as extraordinary as you are.

  3. 03

    Engagement & couples sessions

    A warm-up before the wedding or a reason on its own. Multiple cameras, real candids and true editorial frames, in the places that already mean something to you.

  4. 04

    Planning assistance

    Location and activity scouting, a customized timeline built around light, permit assistance and vendor recommendations. I scout the timing so the best hour of the day is already yours.

  5. 05

    Outfit & styling consulting

    I’m a big fashion girlie and I loooove contributing to outfit selections. Send me the options. I’ll tell you what photographs beautifully and fix the stray hair before you ever see it.

  6. 06

    Full-day storytelling coverage

    Coffee at the diner through the last champagne pour. I document the whole arc of the day so the gallery reads like a story rather than a list of moments.

  7. 07

    Print & album design

    Your photos deserve to live somewhere other than a phone. I design heirloom albums and prints so these memories are cherished for generations.
