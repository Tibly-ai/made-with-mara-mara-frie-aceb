Meet your photographer

Hi, I’m Mara. Your favorite West‑Coast New Yorker.

I fell in love with photography through my love for both art and socializing. My profession is more than adjusting a few buttons; it’s about the relationships I form while doing so. I love people. I consider every shoot I book to be its own personal adventure.

The opportunity to preserve your special memories for you to look back on for the rest of your life is so rewarding and so humbling. It’s why I do what I do. Born and raised in Cleveland, Arizona State for college, then LA, and I pay rent in NYC today.

How we’ll tell your story

You’re getting so much more than a photographer.

A super excited planner, travel guide, stylist, bouquet holder, your own personal hype‑woman, adventure buddy, and more.

01
Elopement photography
For couples ready to break tradition and do things their way. Run through the NYC streets or hike the mountains in Malibu; no matter the season, we’ll build the day around you.
02
Intimate wedding photography
Small guest lists, city hall vows, a few favorite people. Days about connection rather than convention, photographed with luxury aesthetic and a lot of laughing.
03
Engagement sessions
Cherry blossoms in Riverside Park, the marble halls of Grand Central, or your own block. We pick the spot and the light together.
04
Couples photography
The raw, unabashed connection between two souls. No occasion required, just an afternoon and the two of you.
05
Planning assistance
Location and activity scouting, a customized timeline, permit assistance, and vendor recommendations. There are a lot of options out there; this is where I come in to guide you.
06
Outfit & styling consulting
I’m a big fashion girlie and I loooove contributing to outfit selections. I also pay close attention to detail, so no stray hair is sticking up on my watch.
07
Full-day storytelling coverage
From bottomless diner coffee in the morning to the last champagne pour at night, documented as it actually happened.
08
Print & album design
Memories that will be cherished for generations belong somewhere other than a phone. We’ll design prints and albums together after your gallery arrives.
