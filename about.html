A little about me

Hi, I’m
Mara

aka your favorite West-Coast New Yorker

Awkward doesn’t exist here. I fell in love with photography through my love for both art and socializing. My profession is more than just adjusting a few buttons; it’s about the relationships I form while doing so.

I love people. I consider every shoot I book to be its own personal adventure. The opportunity to preserve your special memories for you to look back on for the rest of your life is so rewarding and so humbling. It’s why I do what I do :)

Some context about me: I was born and raised in Cleveland, went to Arizona State, moved to LA after college, and live in (well…pay rent in) NYC today. I’m an elopement and intimate wedding photographer who specializes in working with couples who value both luxury aesthetic and adventure.

When you work with me you are getting so much more than a photographer. You’re also getting a super excited planner, travel guide, stylist, bouquet holder, your own personal hype-woman, adventure buddy, and more.

My style

timeless nostalgic chic bold

When we are shooting together, the only thing that I’m inspired by is you. Nothing I create is executed to fit in a box. When I’m creating, all I think about is how to capture the way a moment feels. Let’s document the real stuff.
